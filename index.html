THE HEART OF RAFIKI

A sound with meaning. A generation with purpose.

Rafiki Zespół is a gospel music band and faith-driven movement dedicated to transforming lives through worship and purpose.

Rooted in Christ, we exist to make His love known and inspire young people to discover meaning, identity and joy through the power of music and ministry.
