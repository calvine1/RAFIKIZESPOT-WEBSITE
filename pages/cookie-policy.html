This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the Rafiki Zespół website. We aim to be transparent about the technologies used and the choices available to visitors.

02

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit. They can help a website remember preferences, support certain features and understand how visitors interact with its pages.

03

Depending on the features active on the website, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:

03

Support content or services provided by third parties.

Essential cookies

Optional cookies

Analytics, preference or third-party cookies may be used where applicable and in accordance with relevant consent requirements.

05

Third-party services

Our website may link to or include content from services such as YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp. When you interact with those services, their providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

06

Your choices and managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or remove those already stored on your device.

Blocking some cookies may affect how certain websites or embedded services work. Where consent is legally required, optional cookies should not be activated before the appropriate consent is obtained.
