Our Heart

Faith That Connects Us. Love That Moves Us.

Rafiki Zespół is more than a music team. We are a community brought together by faith, a shared love for worship, and a desire to make our gifts count for something greater than ourselves.

We value genuine relationships, encouraging one another, serving people, and creating opportunities for our faith to be expressed through practical acts of love.

Our guiding Scripture — Acts 13:36

A reminder of David serving God's purpose in his own generation, inspiring our commitment to serve faithfully in ours.
