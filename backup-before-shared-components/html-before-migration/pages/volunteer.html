Serve With Purpose

Your gifts can make a difference.

Rafiki Zespół is more than music. We are a community committed to serving God's purpose through worship, creativity and outreach. Bring your skills, your time and your heart, and help us make an impact.

Become a Volunteer ↗
Find Your Place

There is a place for you.

You do not have to be on stage to make a difference. Whether your strength is creativity, organisation, technology or practical service, your contribution can help move the vision forward.

♫

Music & Worship

Contribute your musical ability, vocals, instrumental skills or worship experience to our ministry and events.

▣

Media & Photography

Help capture moments, create content, manage social media and share the Rafiki story through digital platforms.

✦

Creative & Design

Use graphic design, video editing, writing and creative communication to give our message a strong visual identity.

⚙

Technical Support

Support sound, lighting, equipment, live production and other technical needs during rehearsals, services and events.

◎

Events & Administration

Help coordinate programmes, organise logistics, welcome guests, manage communication and support the team behind the scenes.

♡

Outreach & Practical Help

Take part in community initiatives, youth empowerment, charity activities and practical service to people who need support.

Every Contribution Matters

Serve with what you have.

We believe that service is not limited to one particular skill or role. You can contribute your time, professional expertise, equipment, resources or financial support according to your ability and willingness.

If you have a skill that is not listed above, we would still love to hear from you. Let us explore how your contribution can support the mission of Rafiki Zespół.

Ready to get involved?

Tell us a little about yourself, the skills you would like to offer and how you hope to serve. Our team will connect with you to discuss the next steps.
