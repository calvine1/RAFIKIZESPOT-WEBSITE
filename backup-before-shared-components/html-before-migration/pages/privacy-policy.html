Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

01 Introduction

Rafiki Zespół (“Rafiki”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, subscribe to our communications, register for activities, attend our events, interact with our ministry, or purchase merchandise through our website.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with the Constitution of Kenya, the Data Protection Act, 2019, the applicable Data Protection Regulations, and other relevant laws. Visitors outside Kenya may also have rights under the laws applicable to them.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read thisPolicy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately and appropriately. Visiting the website does not automatically constitute consent to every form of data processing described here.

02 Who We Are

Rafiki Zespół is a Christian music and worship ministry that uses music, creative expression, fellowship, events, outreach and digital media to share its message and serve communities.

For privacy enquiries, requests concerning personal information, or concerns about photographs and videos, contact us at:

Privacy enquiries

Rafiki Zespół

rafikizespol24@gmail.com

In this Policy, “personal information” or “personal data” means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

03 Personal Information We May Collect

Depending on the services and features you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

This may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Messages, questions, feedback or invitation requests.
  • Newsletter subscription preferences.
  • Information submitted through registration forms or account creation.
  • Delivery details and other information necessary to fulfil merchandise orders.
  • Records of your communications and requests to us.

Please provide only information that is necessary for the relevant purpose.

3.2 Account and Transaction Information

If website accounts or merchandise purchasing facilities are available, we may process account details, order histories, billing or delivery information, and transaction records.

Payments may be handled by an external payment service provider. Where payment processing is outsourced, the provider may collect and process payment details under its own applicable privacy terms. We will not intentionally retain full payment card security details unless legally permitted and necessary for a properly secured payment arrangement.

3.3 Technical Information and Cookies

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, depending on the website configuration. This may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Browser, device and operating-system information.
  • Pages viewed, referring websites and visit timestamps.
  • Website performance and diagnostic information.
  • Cookie identifiers and analytics information.

We may use essential cookies to support website functionality. Non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies will be used subject to applicable consent requirements.

You can manage cookies through available website controls and your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

3.4 Photographs, Videos and Audio Recordings

Rafiki may take or commission photographs, video recordings and audio recordings at worship services, concerts, rehearsals, community activities, outreach programmes and other events.

These materials may identify individuals directly or indirectly. Identifiable images and recordings may therefore constitute personal data.

We may also receive photographs or other media from authorised photographers, event partners, participants or third-party contributors. We will assess the relevant permissions and legal basis before publishing identifiable material.

3.5 Children’s Personal Information

Our website and events may be accessible to children and young people. We recognise that children’s personal information requires particular care.

Where required by law, we will obtain consent from a parent or guardian before processing a child’s personal data, including for identifiable photography or promotional use. We will consider the child’s best interests, dignity and privacy when deciding whether and how to publish content involving them.

We will not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents and guardians may contact us with concerns or requests concerning a child’s information.

04 How We Use Personal Information

Subject to applicable law, we may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and invitation requests.
  • Communicate event dates, venue details and changes.
  • Administer registrations and participation in activities.
  • Send newsletters or ministry updates where the required consent or other lawful basis exists.
  • Create and manage website accounts, where available.
  • Process merchandise orders, arrange delivery, handle returns and provide customer support.
  • Maintain transaction records and meet legal or accounting obligations.
  • Publish authorised event photographs, videos, testimonies and outreach stories.
  • Promote Rafiki’s ministry, events, music and merchandise where appropriate permissions and legal requirements have been satisfied.
  • Understand website usage, improve performance and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorised access and other security incidents.
  • Protect our legal rights and comply with lawful requests.

We will not use personal data for a materially different purpose in a manner inconsistent with applicable law.

05 Legal Grounds for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal information on one ormore of the following grounds:

  • Your valid consent.
  • The necessity of taking steps at your request or performing a contract, such as fulfilling an order.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Legitimate interests, where lawful, proportionate and not overridden by your rights.
  • Another lawful basis recognised by applicable legislation.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it subject to the law and the practical limits of the relevant processing. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

06 Event Photography and Promotional Materials

Rafiki may document its activities to preserve memories, communicate its mission and promote future events.

Where photography or recording takes place, we aim to provide clear notices and explain the intended uses. Depending on the event and the nature of the recording, we may use suitable notices,registration disclosures, consent forms or other appropriate measures.

Attendance alone will not be treated as unlimited permission for every future use of a person’s image. Where identifiable images are to be used for promotional, commercial or other purposes requiring consent, we will obtain appropriate consent or establish another lawful basis.

Where consent is relied upon, the intended uses may include:

  • Rafiki’s website and official social media channels.
  • Event posters, promotional graphics and announcements.
  • Recaps, ministry reports and outreach publications.
  • Future event promotions and related marketing materials.

We will take particular care with children, vulnerable individuals, sensitive circumstances and close-up promotional portraits.

If you do not wish to be photographed, please inform an event organiser before recording takes place so reasonable arrangements can be discussed. We cannot guarantee that every person will be excluded from every incidental background image, but we will consider reasonablerequests.

You may request removal or restriction of a published image by contacting rafikizespol24@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate action where required by law or otherwise reasonably warranted. Removal from our website may not immediately remove copies previously shared, downloaded, cached or reposted by other people or platforms.

07 Newsletters and Direct Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletters or updates, we may use your contact details to send information about Rafiki’s music, events, ministry activities, merchandise and related announcements, subject to applicable consent and direct-marketing requirements.

You can unsubscribe through an available unsubscribe link or by emailing rafikizespol24@gmail.com.

Unsubscribing from promotional communications will not necessarily stop essential messages concerning an order, account, event registration or another service you have requested.

08 Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell personal information as a routine business practice.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Payment processors and transaction service providers.
  • Delivery and fulfilment providers.
  • Newsletter and communications platforms.
  • Website analytics providers, where lawfully used.
  • Event partners or authorised photographers where necessary for an agreed purpose.
  • Professional advisers or public authorities where disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect lawful rights.

Service providers should receive only the information reasonably necessary for their assigned functions and should be subject to appropriate contractual, confidentiality and security safeguards.

We may also disclose information where you have validly authorised us to do so or where another lawful basis applies.

09 International Data Transfers

Some of our website, hosting, payment, analytics or communications providers may process information outside Kenya.

Where personal data is transferred across borders, we will take the steps required by applicable law, including ensuring appropriate safeguards and obtaining consent where legally required.

International visitors should be aware that information may be processed in a country whose data protection laws differ from those in their country of residence.

10 Data Security

We aim to protect personal information using appropriate technical and organisational measures. Depending on the systems we use, these may include access restrictions, secure connections, authentication controls, backups and service-provider safeguards.

No website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess it and take the notifications and remedial steps required by applicable law.

11 How Long We Retain Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The nature of an enquiry or account.
  • Newsletter preferences and withdrawal requests.
  • Event administration and consent records.
  • Accounting, tax and transaction obligations.
  • The resolution of disputes or legal claims.
  • The need to maintain evidence of permissions for published content.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention obligations and reasonable technical limitations.

12 Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is used.
  • Request access to personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Request deletion of personal data in circumstances recognised by law.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request restriction of processing where legally available.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise applicable rights concerning data portability and automated decision-making.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya or another competent authority, as applicable.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. Some information may need to be retained to comply with law or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

To submit a request, email rafikizespol24@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Request” and explain the information or processing concerned. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

For a photograph or video request, include the event name or date, a description or link to the material, and the action you are requesting.

13 Third-Party Websites and Embedded Content

Our website may link to or embed content from third-party platforms, including video-sharing services and social media networks.

Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not control their independent processing practices.

Please review the privacy policies of the relevant platforms before interacting with embedded content or external links.

14 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our activities, technology or legal obligations. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Where required by law, we will notifyaffected individuals or seek renewed consent before implementing material changes.
