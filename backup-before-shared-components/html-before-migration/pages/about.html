OUR STORY · OUR PURPOSE

More than music.
A movement with purpose.

We are Rafiki Zespół — a gospel music band and faith-driven movement committed to making Christ known and inspiring our generation through music, worship and service.

WHO WE ARE

Where Music Meets Purpose.

Rafiki Zespół is a gospel music band and faith-driven movement dedicated to transforming lives through worship and purpose.

Rooted in Christ, we exist to make His love known and to inspire young people to discover meaning, identity and joy through the power of music and ministry.

We believe music can do more than entertain. It can encourage hearts, strengthen faith, unite people and create opportunities to serve others. This conviction shapes our worship, our relationships and our commitment to the generation we serve.

OUR BIBLICAL FOUNDATION · ACTS 13:36
“For when David had served God's purpose in his own generation, he fell asleep.”

We are inspired by a life lived in service to God's purpose. Our desire is to faithfully serve our generation through music, worship, love, fellowship and meaningful action.
