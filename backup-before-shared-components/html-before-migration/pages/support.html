Simple Steps

How to contribute through M-Pesa.

Use the Till number above and follow the payment prompts on your phone.

1

Open M-Pesa

Open the M-Pesa menu on your phone and choose Lipa na M-Pesa.

2

Select Buy Goods

Choose Buy Goods and Services, then enter Till number 5329392.

3

Confirm Your Payment

Enter your intended contribution, check the displayed recipient details and confirm only when everything is correct.
