This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used on the Rafiki Zespół website and the choices available to visitors.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. They can help websites remember preferences, support functionality and understand how visitors use a website.

3. How we may use cookies

Rafiki Zespół may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality and navigation.
  • Remembering visitor preferences where applicable.
  • Understanding website performance and visitor activity if analytics tools are introduced.
  • Supporting embedded content or services provided by third parties.

4. Essential and optional cookies

Essential cookies may be needed for certain website functions. Optional analytics, preference or third-party cookies should only be used in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

We do not currently promise that every category listed above is active on this website. The technologies actually used may change as the website develops.

5. Third-party services

Some pages may link to or embed services such as YouTube, Instagram or WhatsApp. Those providers may use their own cookies or tracking technologies when you interact with their services. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

6. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain websites or embedded services work. Where consent is legally required, optional cookies should not be activated before the appropriate consent is obtained.
